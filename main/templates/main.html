Furniture Glossary

Cabinet
A piece of furniture with shelves, drawers, or doors, used for storage. Usually placed in kitchens, bathrooms, or living rooms.
Sideboard
A flat-topped piece of furniture used for storing tableware and linens. Traditionally placed in dining rooms.
Wardrobe
A tall cabinet used for hanging clothes and storing personal items. Can be freestanding or built-in.
Ottoman
A low, padded stool without a back or arms. Used as a footrest, seat, or small table.
